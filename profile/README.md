### Our story
Handwerksangriff had it's roots in a survival server created by a few friends in the first half of 2020 for the sole purpose of surviving the boringness of the lockdown restrictions of the corona pandemic. 
After cramming 3 unnamed Minecraft Projects into the first half of 2020, we switched to a yearly cycle starting a few days before Christmas.
It's name is a reference to (or more like a one to one translation of) the biggest German Streamer and YouTuber SMP "CraftAttack".

`2026` will be the `7th` official Handwerksangriff Season.

### What can I find here?
As the technical aspect of the project keept growing, I decided, there is need for an organisation that keeps everything related to Handwerksangriff in one place.
Also I'm a huge fan of OpenSource software, hence most of our custom stuff is available for anyone to use and modify.
